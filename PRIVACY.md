# Privacy Policy — Home Base: Plans & Habits

**Last updated:** [fill in submission date]

Home Base does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data,
because it doesn't need to — the app has no account system, no backend
server, and no network connection of any kind.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter into Home Base — events, quotes, tasks, habits,
alerts, thoughts, your name, and your theme preferences — is saved only
on your own device, using Apple's standard on-device local storage
(AsyncStorage). None of it is ever sent anywhere, backed up to a server,
or made accessible to anyone but you.

## What the app does NOT do

- No account or sign-in of any kind
- No analytics, tracking, or usage data collection
- No advertising, and no third-party ad networks
- No data sharing with any third party, for any reason
- No network requests at all during normal use

## Backups

Home Base includes an optional backup feature (Settings → Data) that
lets you export your data to a file you control. That file is created
and shared entirely through your device's own share sheet — where it
goes (Files, email, AirDrop, etc.) is entirely your choice. Home Base
itself never uploads this file anywhere.

## Children's Privacy

Home Base does not knowingly collect any information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect information from
anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted here with a new
"Last updated" date above.
